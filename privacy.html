Privacy Policy for HybridTrainr™

  • This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information of its users.
  • Workout entries are stored locally on your device and synced via iCloud. Your data is encrypted as per standard Apple practices.
  • Third party services: Cloud synchronization is provided via Apple iCloud. Your data is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy and end-to-end encryption standards.
  • The developer does not have access to your data.
  • No analytics or tracking are used in this app. The graphical insights in the app are calculated locally on-device and on-demand.
  • Payment is handled via RevenueCat and is subject to RevenueCat's privacy policy.
